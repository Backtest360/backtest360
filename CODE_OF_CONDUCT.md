# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the `backtest360` project to be a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful and considerate in all project spaces (issues and any
other communication channels). Behaviour that is harassing, abusive, or
otherwise unwelcome will not be tolerated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual
is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to the maintainers
at **hello@backtest360.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and security
of the reporter of any incident.

## Attribution

version 2.1, available at
